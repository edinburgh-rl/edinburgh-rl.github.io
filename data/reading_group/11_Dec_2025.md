speakers: Trevor McInroe
title: The Underappreciated Third Kind of RL Benchmark
abstract: As RL researchers, we often view environments as a means to isolate a single research challenge. But what if we care about an algorithm's potential real-world ability, where many challenges exist simultaneously? This talk is an open discussion on this idea and introduces Terra Nova, a new environment based on the popular game Civilization V, that embraces this messiness.

bio: Trevor's PhD research focuses on the pathways to getting reinforcement learning into the real world, including offline settings (and variations therein), representation learning for high-dimensional observation spaces, model-based methods, and off-policy methods. He is advised by Amos Storkey and is a member of the Bayesian and Neural Systems lab.
